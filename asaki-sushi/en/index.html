A new sushi bar in Carnaxide, made with care

Asaki Sushi opened in August 2026 on Rua Fernão Lopes in Carnaxide, a few minutes from Lisbon. The idea is simple: all you can eat sushi with the care of an à la carte kitchen, every piece prepared to order and served at your table.

From a quick weekday lunch to a weekend family dinner, there is room for first timers and for regulars who already know what to order.

  • Fresh fishSelected daily.
  • Premium ingredientsQuality and origin.
  • Japanese traditionTechnique and dedication.
  • Refined settingComfort and elegance.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the all you can eat at Asaki Sushi?

Lunch from Tuesday to Friday is €17.90 per person. Dinner, weekends and public holidays are €22.90 per person.

Do children pay full price?

No. Children aged 4 to 9 pay half price.

Does Asaki offer takeaway and delivery?

Yes. Call 210 133 588 to order for pick up or for delivery to your home.

What are the opening hours?

Tuesday to Sunday, 12:00 to 15:00 and 19:00 to 23:00. Closed on Mondays.

How do I book a table?
